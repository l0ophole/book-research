# INC052 — Minot AFB, ND: LFs O-6, N-7, Oscar/November LCFs, Bowbells area; B-52 inbound (5th BW)

- **Date:** 1968-10-24
- **Significance:** CRITICAL · **Status:** PARTIALLY_VERIFIED (confidence 3/5) · **Basis:** DOCUMENT_AND_WITNESS
- **Provenance:** Chapter 15 — 15. “Someone is seeing flying saucers again.” (SEC080), book line 12773

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Camper/maintenance teams and Base Ops log report bright maneuvering object near N-7; weather radar tracks object near Bowbells; B-52 radar paced by return 'as big or bigger than a KC-135' for ~20 mi, UHF transmitters disrupted; radarscope camera 14 frames; B-52 directed over ground-hovering oval orange object with tube and crescent; Blue Book case 12,548.

## Competing explanations
- [EXP024](../competing_explanations/EXP024.md) Misidentification of Sirius, Vega, and the B-52 itself (Base Ops dispatcher log: lights correlate with B-52 VOR penetration, low approach, missed approach); anomalous propagation/ball-lightning-like plasma (Blue Book); radar scope artefacts
- [EXP025](../competing_explanations/EXP025.md) Unrelated physical intrusion at Oscar-7 LF (padlock open, combination dial moved) at 0449 CDT — possible human intruder activity; distractor or cause of alarm
- [EXP026](../competing_explanations/EXP026.md) B-52 itself (upper airwork, VOR penetration, low approach with landing lights, missed approach) plus haze/inversion explaining many ground sightings (Blue Book discussion)
- [EXP027](../competing_explanations/EXP027.md) Radar artefacts/natural: meteor echo, anomalous propagation, multiple-trip returns, lightning, ECM/other B-52 radar interference, balloon, satellite, birds/insects (all tested by Shough)
- [EXP028](../competing_explanations/EXP028.md) Sirius under temperature inversion/haze with scintillation, autokinesis, passing cloud (Blue Book Atch)
- [EXP029](../competing_explanations/EXP029.md) Terrestrial light or other aircraft/ground source seen at long range, with range and height misjudged at night in haze (Blue Book offered "a light on the ground" as an alternative to Vega)
- [EXP030](../competing_explanations/EXP030.md) Unauthorised entry by someone holding a padlock key, e.g., a current/former Air Police member (three earlier similar incidents were so traced); not connected to the sightings
- [EXP031](../competing_explanations/EXP031.md) Internal malfunction of the B-52 radar/transmitter or an inversion-induced anomalous return (Quintanilla, 30 Oct)
