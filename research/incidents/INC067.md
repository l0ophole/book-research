## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
TSgt John W. Mills III (then A1C, combat targeting team) and 2Lt team chief, with a topside guard, report a loud low-frequency hum felt through the body, a black region overhead with a straight edge and ~60-70 deg corners (rhombus), no stars inside it; site lights, truck and radio died, G&C No-Go; object gone when power returned; command post/job control angry; teams told to say nothing; two other teams (Echo, third unnamed) reported similar; hearsay that ~12-13 missiles (all Echo, part of Delta incl. D-1) went down.
