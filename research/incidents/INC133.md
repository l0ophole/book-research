## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Dunn (K-9, 42nd SPS): intense white light shone at ~45 degrees onto the WSA area commander's truck cab (green glow), 5-15 s, silent; two F-106s overflew 4-5 min later after a radar bogey; both men felt it was non-threatening and 'alien'; no debrief. Sergeant unnamed.
