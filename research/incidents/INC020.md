# INC020 — Oscar Flight (LCF Oscar), Malmstrom AFB, MT

- **Date:** 1967-03-24 (probable; uncertain)
- **Significance:** CRITICAL · **Status:** DISPUTED (confidence 2/5) · **Basis:** WITNESS_NARRATIVE
- **Provenance:** Chapter 11 — 11. Taking Down Echo and Oscar (SEC073), book line 9904

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Salas/Meiwald: guards report glowing red saucer hovering at LCF front gate; guard injured approaching; then 6-8 missiles go No-Go at once, mostly G&C. OSI debrief, NDAs signed, alert logs confiscated. Air Force records do not acknowledge the incident.

- [WIT011](../witnesses/WIT011.md) Capt. (then 1Lt) Robert Salas — YES (Oscar); PARTIAL (Echo)
- [WIT012](../witnesses/WIT012.md) Lt. (later Col.) Fred Meiwald — YES
- [WIT021](../witnesses/WIT021.md) Jim Klotz — INVESTIGATOR

## Competing explanations
- [EXP010](../competing_explanations/EXP010.md) Misdated/conflated memory; shutdown at Oscar may be the same as Echo event; guard misperception of a helicopter, lights or aircraft
- [EXP011](../competing_explanations/EXP011.md) Electronic/EM effect from a secret test or Soviet device (unspecified)
- [EXP039](../competing_explanations/EXP039.md) Conflation: Salas and Meiwald experienced a separate, minor event (UFO sighting by guards at Oscar, no outage) and Salas later merged it with the documented Echo shutdown; or Echo electrical fault only, no UFO
- [EXP040](../competing_explanations/EXP040.md) Two separate observations were merged: Meiwald team-at-LF report (UFO seen from patrol; minor security alert) and the Salas gate object; plus missile faults electrical; both recollections 44 years later, with Salas leading the 1996 call
