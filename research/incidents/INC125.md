# INC125 — Bentwaters air traffic control tower

- **Date:** 1980-12-26 to 1981-01-01 (night; ~2200-2400 per Carey, before 0325 GMT 28 Dec per Eastern Radar)
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** TESTIMONY_ONLY
- **Provenance:** Chapter 21 — 21. Beams of Light (SEC087), book line 17110

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Controllers Carey and Barker report a very fast radar target crossing a 60-mile scope (~120 miles in 8-12 s), returning, and (Carey only) a right-angle turn; Barker (not Carey) also saw an orange basketball-shaped object hover over the water tower, with six to eight light 'portholes'; no log entry; Eastern Radar's log (0325 GMT 28 Dec) records a call about a 'flashing light' over Rendlesham Forest, with nothing seen on radar.
