# INC130 — Loring AFB, Maine, WSA and flight line

- **Date:** 1975-10-27 1945 to 1975-10-28 (same hour)
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** PARTIALLY_VERIFIED (confidence 3/5) · **Basis:** DOCUMENT_BASED
- **Provenance:** Chapter 18 — END OF NORAD LOG ENTRIES (SEC084), book line 14525

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
27 Oct about 1945: low craft (~300 ft, circling, within 300 yd of WSA at ~150 ft), SP Danny K. Lewis, base alerted, messages to NMCC/CSAF/SAC; described as 'helicopter' in message traffic. 28 Oct: Sgt Steven Eichner (B-52 crew chief) says elongated reddish-orange football 'as long as four cars' hovered near WSA, 5 ft up, 300 ft from his truck; sworn affidavit. Sams (2003) says .50-cal jeeps from Pease were to be fetched but the flight was cancelled.
