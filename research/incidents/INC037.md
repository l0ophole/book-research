# INC037 — Walker AFB, NM - 579th SMS Atlas Site 9 (west of Roswell)

- **Date:** 1962-63 or winter 1963/64
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** WITNESS_NARRATIVE
- **Provenance:** Chapter 7 — The Other Roswell Incidents (SEC044), book line 5538

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Repeated (3-10) occasions of silent objects hovering over silo and shining lights; guards frightened (Nelson DMCCC from launch capsule, did not see). Goss (guard) saw erratic fast lights twice, 2-3 weeks apart, winter 1963-64; told by launch commander they were military aircraft (once per SAC HQ duty officer).
