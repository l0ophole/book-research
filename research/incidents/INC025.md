# INC025 — Big Sur, CA telescope site / Vandenberg AFB (Atlas D RV test) / Pacific

- **Date:** 1964-09 (candidate dates 2, 3, 15 Sept per Jacobs log; Hastings favors 15 Sept 1964)
- **Significance:** CRITICAL · **Status:** DISPUTED (confidence 2/5) · **Basis:** WITNESS_NARRATIVE
- **Provenance:** Chapter 9 — 9. Shot from the Sky (SEC055), book line 7439

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Lt. Bob Jacobs (1369th Photo Sqn, OIC Photo Instrumentation) says Image Orthicon telescope film of Atlas ICBM re-entry vehicle test showed domed disc UFO circle the dummy warhead, emit four flashes, warhead tumbled and fell hundreds of miles short of Eniwetok; film shown once or thrice at Vandenberg, then confiscated by 'agents'.
