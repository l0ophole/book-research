# INC132 — Wurtsmith AFB WSA

- **Date:** unknown date, Jan 1975 - Aug 1976 (Erikson assigned then)
- **Significance:** MEDIUM · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** WITNESS_NARRATIVE
- **Provenance:** Chapter 18 — END OF NORAD LOG ENTRIES (SEC084), book line 14652

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
'John Erikson' (anonymous K-9 SP) says SP radio chatter reported a bright silent light hovering over the WSA, he could not see it, F-106s from K.I. Sawyer were scrambled and he heard no jets, SPs told not to discuss. Hastings thinks this may be a different event than 30 Oct 1975 but says no document supports it.
