# INC123 — RAF Bentwaters WSA tower (via 2164th Comm Sq repairman Thompson)

- **Date:** 1980-12 (Thompson: nights of ~25-26 and ~27-28 Dec)
- **Significance:** MEDIUM · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** HEARSAY
- **Provenance:** Chapter 21 — 21. Beams of Light (SEC087), book line 16244

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Thompson (secondhand from a coworker in the WSA tower) says a very bright light hovered briefly near the tower and left toward Woodbridge (first night) and on a second night buzzed the tower low enough that two men 'hit the deck' and dropped into the trees; the coworker was later briefed, told they had not seen anything, and threatened by (presumed) OSI; a coworker said the Light-Alls, truck engines and radios failed in the forest on 27/28 Dec.
