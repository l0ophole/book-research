# INC124 — RAF Bentwaters WSA (81st SPS D Flight)

- **Date:** 1980-12 (Bobo: Halt night; Waters: "that same week")
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** TESTIMONY_ONLY
- **Provenance:** Chapter 21 — 21. Beams of Light (SEC087), book line 16393

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Bobo (WSA tower): large slightly oblong light with bluish/reddish tint hovered ~5 h over the forest, then shot off four or five small lights and vanished; no beams; no debrief. Waters (ART): spinning multicolored cantaloupe-size light descended slowly toward the forest, no beams, burn marks on trees reported next morning. Egercic (alarm monitor with the radio net): never heard of a UFO or beams at the WSA; a beam falling on the bunkers would have been a security breach ('Covered Wagon').
