# INC013 — Nevada Proving Ground (Yucca Flat), Buster-Jangle Charlie

- **Date:** 1951-10-30
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** WITNESS_NARRATIVE
- **Provenance:** Chapter 3 — SQUADRON OF DISCS OVER ATOMIC TEST SITE OCTOBER 30, 1951 (SEC024), book line 2470

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Anonymous 'Mr. M.' (USAF airman from Nellis, sentry duty) reports 18 silvery domed discs in formation hovering 30-60 sec ~15-20 min before 14-kt Charlie shot; Sgt of the Guard said 'If we're smart, we won't say anything.' Walter Webb interviewed 1981 (phone), dated it by yield/sunrise.
