# INC128 — Malmstrom AFB Weapons Storage Area (east side of base), Montana

- **Date:** 1975 fall, before 31 Oct 1975 (Chassey left the USAF Oct 1975); date unknown
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** HEARSAY
- **Provenance:** Chapter 18 — END OF NORAD LOG ENTRIES (SEC084), book line 14254

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Chassey (secondhand, overheard SP radio call at midnight chow): unknown intruder over the WSA. Rumor next day: Malmstrom helicopters chased an extremely bright light east toward Belt and it returned to base, outrunning them. Peisher (Det 5, 37th ARS, helicopter CO) says 'that sounds right' but learned of it only 'much later'.
