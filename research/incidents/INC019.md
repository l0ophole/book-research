# INC019 — Echo Flight, Malmstrom AFB, MT (341st SMW)

- **Date:** 1967-03-16
- **Significance:** CRITICAL · **Status:** PARTIALLY_VERIFIED (confidence 3/5) · **Basis:** DOCUMENT_AND_WITNESS
- **Provenance:** Chapter 11 — 11. Taking Down Echo and Oscar (SEC073), book line 9871

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
All 10 Minuteman I missiles went No-Go ~0830 due to guidance & control faults; UFOs reportedly seen by maintenance teams at two widely separated LFs and confirmed by Security Alert Team before dawn. Official wing history: UFO rumors 'disproven'. Boeing investigators duplicated faults by injecting 'noise' into logic coupler; source unknown.
