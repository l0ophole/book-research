# INC054 — Minot AFB missile field, ND

- **Date:** 1966-08-24
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** NOT_YET_INVESTIGATED (confidence 0/5) · **Basis:** PUBLISHED_SECONDARY
- **Provenance:** Chapter 10 — Hynek Opens the Door (SEC068), book line 9402

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Hynek (SEP, Dec 1966) summarized a preliminary AF report: launch control radio disrupted by static; guards saw a UFO that appeared to land; strike-team radio also disrupted ~10 miles from landing site; object rose and passed beneath a second identical object; Minot radar tracked both. Fowler later told the Christian Science Monitor (5 Dec 1973); the Air Force said SAC unit histories showed nothing.
