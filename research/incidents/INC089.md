# INC089 — Roswell Army Air Field and Foster ranch area, NM (Lincoln/Chaves counties)

- **Date:** 1947-07 (debris found by ~2-3 Jul; press release 8 Jul 1947)
- **Significance:** MEDIUM · **Status:** DISPUTED (confidence 2/5) · **Basis:** TESTIMONY_AND_DOCUMENT
- **Provenance:** Chapter 27 — 27. Roswell (SEC094), book line 20196

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Roswell: debris found by Mack Brazel, examined by Maj. Jesse Marcel, press release of a recovered 'flying disc' on 8 Jul 1947, Ramey reversal to 'weather balloon' at Fort Worth; later claims of a crashed spacecraft and bodies (Lytle, Exon, Haut affidavit). Hastings stresses an atomic-weapons link (509th BG, Trinity proximity) but says he is not a Roswell researcher.
