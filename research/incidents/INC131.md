# INC131 — Wurtsmith AFB, Michigan (WSA, Lake Huron, Saginaw Bay)

- **Date:** 1975-10-30 about 2210 to 2300 local (NORAD log: incident 0355Z 31 Oct)
- **Significance:** HIGH · **Status:** PARTIALLY_VERIFIED (confidence 3/5) · **Basis:** DOCUMENT_BASED
- **Provenance:** Chapter 18 — END OF NORAD LOG ENTRIES (SEC084), book line 14601

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Several SPs report a low craft with running lights, then a lightless craft hovering over the WSA ~5 min; RAPCON tracks low objects SE; KC-135 (Taylor) vectored to intercept sees flashing lights, 1 radar paint ~10 s, object recedes ('approx 1000 knots'). NORAD log calls it a helicopter; FBI/police notified; Michigan DNR had no aircraft up. Per NICAP/Clear Intent, no explanation was ever given.
