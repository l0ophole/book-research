# INC116 — Malmstrom AFB, MT

- **Date:** 1966 early spring (Fowler); Jan 1966-Aug 1967 (Hughes)
- **Significance:** MEDIUM · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** HEARSAY
- **Provenance:** Chapter 10 — Another Shutdown at Malmstrom AFB (SEC070), book line 9665

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Fowler (secondhand from a former launch officer who joined Sylvania) says a whole flight of ten Minuteman missiles shut down as a UFO was sighted near the LCF in early spring 1966 (flight not named). Air Policeman Hughes (Foxtrot LCF) saw a light between Choteau and Augusta moving at incredible speeds and right-angle turns, was told 'Telstar', and heard of jets scrambled after a radar echo (later denied).
