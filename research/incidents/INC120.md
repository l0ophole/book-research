# INC120 — Oscar Flight and India Flight, Malmstrom

- **Date:** 1967-03 (Jamison: Oscar night; Hastings: 24/25 Mar) and ~2 weeks later (India)
- **Significance:** CRITICAL · **Status:** UNVERIFIED (confidence 1/5) · **Basis:** TESTIMONY_ONLY

## Description (as presented in the book; paraphrase)
Lt Jamison (Combat Targeting Team) was called late at night to restart Oscar; rumor and an NCO said guards had reported a UFO before the 10 missiles went down; teams held in the hangar 2-3 h, then briefed 'UFO activity had been messing things up' and told to close the personnel hatch if they saw one; he overheard a UFO 'in a canyon' near Belt; about two weeks later 4-5 missiles at India went down in daylight.
