# EXP057 — explanation for INC091

**Explanation:** Arcturus low in the west (Junkin, 2100 PDT, 16 Sep 2004: mag -0.05, altitude ~21 deg due west) scintillating through the lower atmosphere, plus a jet or helicopter; other reports are aircraft, helicopters (a Blackhawk is mentioned), flares and fireballs

**Supporting evidence:** Arcturus was the brightest star in the west at about the stated time and place (EVD118); Junkin considered and rejected a bright star

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Two adults and four children saw it; second light flew in and both lights extinguished together
