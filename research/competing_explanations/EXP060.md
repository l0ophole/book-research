# EXP060 — explanation for INC099

**Explanation:** Chance and base rate: with 5-6 test series and ~45 shots, 9-17% of days in 1951-1955 fall within 3-10 days of a shot, and fallout spread across the whole U.S.; meteors (including a very active 1948-51 green-fireball period over NM predating Nevada tests) and observer expectation after publicity

**Supporting evidence:** Base-rate calculation (EVD124); fireballs reported at Los Alamos and Sandia before 1951 where no fallout existed (Wilson himself notes this)

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Wilson claims regional match of fallout trajectories and reports; no systematic data published

**Status:** OPEN (assumption: none of these has been independently tested in this project)
