# EXP067 — explanation for INC130;INC131;INC132;INC133

**Explanation:** Helicopter or other conventional aircraft (hovering, rotor-like sound, running lights, lights off over the WSA) as stated in the NORAD and message traffic; Wurtsmith: no aircraft identified, Michigan DNR had none up. For INC132 and INC133: memory conflation with INC131 (Erikson, 20 years later) and bright planet or aircraft light misjudged at night (Dunn, 30 years later). No evidence found here to test any of these.

**Supporting evidence:** NORAD log and 11 Nov 1975 CINCNORAD message use the word helicopter; Wurtsmith witnesses heard helicopter-like sound; Taylor estimates are rough.

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Taylor reports speed ~1000 kt and 200 kt chase by estimate; radar paint ~10 s; no helicopter identified; DNR denial reported by Clear Intent only; Eichner object not helicopter.
