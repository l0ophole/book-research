# EXP066 — explanation for INC123;INC124;INC125

**Explanation:** Stars and planets low on the horizon (Deneb, Vega N; Jupiter, Saturn ESE-SE; Sirius S-SSW), the Orford Ness lighthouse (bearing east) and its beam as the "stream of light" from the south, a fireball/Cosmos 749 re-entry earlier on 25/26 Dec, and a ground-level light show amplified by radio chatter and expectation

**Supporting evidence:** EVD135: stars at ~10 deg in N and S at Halt's times; Halt himself describes a lighthouse at 30-40 deg to the right; Eastern Radar log shows a flashing light and nothing on radar; all WSA-tower and alarm witnesses deny beams at the WSA

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Halt, Penniston, Burroughs, Bobo, Waters report solid or close objects; radar targets reported by Carey and Barker (unlogged, inconsistent)
