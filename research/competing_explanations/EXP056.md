# EXP056 — explanation for INC090

**Explanation:** Misperception of a bolide/fireball seen that evening (green flashes reported on TV; the green/blue fast object elsewhere) with a separate, closer slow object (aircraft, helicopter or lights) at Bangor; observer-position/size-distance error

**Supporting evidence:** No astronomical object at 2122 PDT could account for a low slow disc in the west (EVD118); the fast green object seen across WA/OR/BC resembles a bolide but the date lacks any fireball record I could find

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Swanson saw detail (ring, dark centre) not reported by others; low altitude over the base only; Hastings cannot place other witnesses
