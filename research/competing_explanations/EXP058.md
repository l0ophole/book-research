# EXP058 — explanation for INC095

**Explanation:** The setting nearly full Moon (97%, alt ~14 deg SSW) or Mars (m-0.5, alt ~9 deg SE) seen with smoke and glow, or a fire plume/glow, at ~0425 MSD; radiation change due to the observers changing position/shielding

**Supporting evidence:** Moon and Mars were both low and orange-tinted (EVD118); instruments and positions undocumented

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Multiple accounts; Pravda article is a 2002 retrospective

**Status:** OPEN (assumption: none of these has been independently tested in this project)
