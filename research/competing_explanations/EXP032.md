# EXP032 — explanation for INC033

**Explanation:** Stars, planets (Jupiter, Capella, Saturn, Vega, Altair), satellites (Echo 2), aircraft and meteors seen through a temperature inversion, with group suggestion/mass reporting once a few airmen reported (Blue Book label "inversion effect"; Printy)

**Supporting evidence:** Reports of 1-2 s colored flashing, star-like sizes, 148 objects from 143 personnel; Jupiter/Aldebaran/Capella in the east-NE sky at 0317-0340 MDT; no positional data, no photos; Hynek-printed log is on the Blue Book (Eastern) clock; under that clock "overhead" = Deneb, "seven/six in the east" = Pleiades, "70 deg azimuth" = Pleiades/Aldebaran, NE = Capella (EVD061)

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Log describes objects moving, stacking and passing through each other, a "UFO 90 miles east at high speed, landed 10 miles east", nine objects in three groups; time zone unresolved; Blue Book gave no case-by-case identification; instrumented theodolite observation; 3:17 flashing-red-light oval, az 120 deg entry, "stacked vertically ... passed through the other two", only two bright NW stars for "four NW" (EVD062); Echo 2 untested
