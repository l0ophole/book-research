# EXP031 — explanation for INC052 (airborne radar echo)

**Explanation:** Internal malfunction of the B-52 radar/transmitter or an inversion-induced anomalous return (Quintanilla, 30 Oct)

**Supporting evidence:** Quintanilla; Goff (TDPA) suggested plasma instead; echoes elongated like a rapidly moving target (Shough)

**Contradicting evidence / problems:** Werlich: radios worked before and after, not a malfunction; discrete moving echoes across 13 scans; Shough: no conventional explanation fully convincing

**Status:** OPEN (assumption: none of these has been independently tested in this project)
