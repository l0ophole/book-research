## Extracted information
Partin (completed 30 Oct): bright orange ball of light at 1 o'clock ~15 mi, on/just above ground, stationary; "looked like a miniature sun placed on the ground below the aircraft"; sketch "orange ball of light" with "a very dim ring of soft white light"; time 0430-0435 CDT annotated "should be 0335"; "Only stars" as illumination. Smith: reddish burnt orange, twinkling like a star, slight green, on horizon. Isley: headlight/landing-light-like lights and green flashing light, two objects at one time. Bond: point of light, fuzzy edges, 2 objects, Mohall town light 4 mi. O'Connor: white ball -> dim green -> dim amber, "stingray".
