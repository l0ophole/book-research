## Extracted information
Frame-by-frame description of scans 771-783; quotes Werlich memo ("2½ mile in 3 sec or about 3,000 mi/hr"); reconstructs the "3900" figure as the average of two paint-time-corrected rates (~1,990 kt and ~5,870 kt co-altitude, relative to B-52; ~3,900 kt); notes 3000 mph cannot be recovered from photos; only ~1/10 of original frames survive; tests meteor, AP, multiple-trip, ECM, balloon, aircraft hypotheses; concludes none fully convincing.
