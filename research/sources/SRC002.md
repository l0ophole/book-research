## Extracted information
At 0845 on 16 Mar 1967 all Echo Flight sites lost strategic alert nearly simultaneously (VRSA channels 9 and 12 No-Go); "normal controlled shutdown"; Engineering Investigation Team report dated 23 Mar 1967; weather ruled out; "Rumors of Unidentified Flying Objects ... were disproven" (Mobile Strike Team checked November Flight LFs, no sightings); 801st Radar Sq negative; E-1 power fluctuation 1453 and commercial power restored 1921 same day; Fergus Electric transformer short at Winifred substation; similar Alpha Flight event 19 Dec 1966; external signals judged "very remote"; further study at contractor facility.
