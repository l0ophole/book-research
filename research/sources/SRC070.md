## Extracted information
Para 1: 27 Dec 80 ~0300L, triangular object in forest; para 2: depressions, 0.1 mR Beta/Gamma on 29 Dec; para 3: later that night a red sun-like light that broke into five, then three star-like objects, two N and one S about 10 deg above the horizon, moving in sharp angular movements with red, green and blue lights; the object to the south beamed down a stream of light from time to time. No mention of the WSA or nuclear weapons.
