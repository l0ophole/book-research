## Extracted information
Warren/Cheyenne cases 31 Jul-2 Aug: many stars/planets and meteors; each report only a few sentences; no positional data; many sightings ~0500Z; Jupiter, Capella, Saturn, Vega, Altair, Betelgeuse involved; satellites (Echo 2) and aircraft; temperature inversion on all three nights; Blue Book labelled the "Midwest flap" "inversion effect"; no photographs or triangulation; once a few airmen reported, "others around the base joined in"; local UFO officer did not follow up. Individual Blue Book entries for Warren: insufficient data (Echo 2 per Printy), stars/planets (Capella, Arcturus, probably Vega).
