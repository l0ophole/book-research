## Extracted information
Addendum describes: civilian guard at Sioux Army Depot reporting to Golf LCF, AF personnel viewing with binoculars at 2145 and 2220 local (two objects ~1 mile apart, North Star size, pale yellow to white, ~30 deg above horizon); a group of nine military personnel with binoculars (star-size object moving west, disappearing and reappearing; another pea-size luminous globe; "on four occasions a roaring jet-like noise at some distance behind"); a three-man Geodetic Survey Team with a 40-power theodolite (object twice a pinhead in the Big Dipper, resolving into three white objects). The Blue Book cover record is "insufficient data".
